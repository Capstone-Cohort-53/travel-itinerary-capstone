Family Trip Organizer Persona

Persona

Name: Penelope Martinez

Age: 41

Trip: Multigenerational family vacation

Group: Spouse, kids, siblings, parents, and grandparents

Penelope is a working professional, wife, and mother who frequently takes the lead when her extended family travels together. She is currently organizing a multigenerational family vacation that includes her spouse and children, siblings and their families, parents, and grandparents.

Penelope loves her family and sees vacations as an opportunity for everyone to spend meaningful time together and create memories. She enjoys researching destinations, finding restaurants and activities, and putting together a trip that has something for everyone. Because she is organized and dependable, she naturally becomes the person responsible for keeping track of the details and making sure everyone knows the plan.

Attitudes and Behaviors

Penelope enjoys planning and puts a lot of thought into creating experiences her family will enjoy. She typically does most of the planning from her laptop, researching activities, making reservations, comparing options, and coordinating schedules.

She wants her family to have input, but she does not necessarily want everyone controlling or changing the itinerary. She may ask the group to choose between restaurants, RSVP to an activity, or decide whether they want to participate in an optional outing.

Once the trip begins, Penelope relies more heavily on her phone and wants quick access to the day's plans. However, because she organized the trip, everyone continues to rely on her for information. Instead of simply enjoying the vacation, she is answering questions about what time they need to leave, where they are going next, who is attending an activity, and where they are meeting.

Penelope also frequently finds herself digging through emails and confirmation messages to locate reservation details, addresses, confirmation numbers, and other important information. By the end of the vacation, everyone has had a great time, but Penelope is exhausted from making sure it all happened.

Frustrations and Needs

Penelope's biggest frustration is becoming the central source of information for the entire trip. Family members repeatedly ask questions like, "What time is dinner?", "Where are we meeting?", "What are we doing tomorrow?", and "Who's going with us?"

Important information is often scattered across emails, reservation confirmations, text messages, notes, and conversations. When plans change, Penelope has to make sure everyone has the most current information.

Planning for multiple generations makes this even more complicated. Her children may want to participate in activities that her parents or grandparents do not, and not every traveler needs to attend every event. Penelope needs an easy way to collect RSVPs or opinions while still maintaining control of the overall itinerary.

She needs one centralized place where she can organize activities, times, locations, reservation information, and participation so her family can find answers without always asking her.

Goals

Penelope wants to create a memorable family vacation without spending the entire trip managing everyone else's experience.

Before the trip, she wants to create a shared itinerary, add travelers, enter activities with dates, times, locations, and important details, and allow family members to RSVP or vote when group input is needed. She wants to remain the primary organizer while making the planning process collaborative when it makes sense.

During the trip, Penelope wants every family member to be able to quickly answer the question, "What are we doing today?" without asking her. They should be able to see where they need to be, when they need to be there, what they are doing, and who is participating.

Most importantly, Penelope wants to put her phone away, be present with her family, and enjoy the vacation she worked so hard to plan.

User Story

As the organizer of a multigenerational family vacation, I need one place to organize and share our itinerary so everyone knows the plan without constantly asking me for updates.
