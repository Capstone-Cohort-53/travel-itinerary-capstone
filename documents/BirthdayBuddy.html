Milestone 1: Birthday Party Site

Capstone Project Documentation

Purpose

The purpose of this site is to give party guests one simple, mobile-friendly place to find everything they need for the birthday party, including the date, time, location, theme, and RSVP, so the host does not have to answer the same questions over and over.

Audience

This site is designed for the friends, family members, and coworkers invited to the birthday party. Most guests will open the site on a smartphone from a text message or social media link. They have a wide range of ages and technical comfort levels, so the site must be easy to read and quick to use.

Goal

The goal is for at least 90% of invited guests to RSVP through the site within one week of receiving the link, and for the host to receive fewer than five follow-up questions about basic party details.

Persona

Name

Maria Delgado

Description

Maria is a 34-year-old elementary school teacher and a close friend of the birthday guest. She uses her iPhone for nearly everything and checks the site in Safari, usually between classes or while commuting. She is friendly and organized, replies to invites quickly, and likes knowing the plan ahead of time. She will open the site from a text message link and expects to find the details in under a minute.

Frustrations

  • Invites that bury the date, time, or address
  • Pages that load slowly or are hard to read on a phone
  • Not knowing whether to bring a gift, food, or a plus-one
  • Having to text the host to ask basic questions

Goals

  • Quickly see when and where the party is
  • RSVP easily
  • Find out the theme, dress code, and gift or registry information

Additional Information Needed

  • Parking or transit directions
  • Food and dietary information, including vegetarian and allergy-friendly options
  • Host contact information for last-minute questions
  • Start and end times so she can arrange a sitter
