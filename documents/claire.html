Persona

Name:

Corporate Claire

Age:

38

Profession:

Corporate event planner responsible for organizing quarterly company retreats, conferences, and team travel for groups of 8–20 coworkers.

Technology:

Corporate worker who regularly uses a laptop (2025 MacBook Air M4) for work and a smartphone (iPhone 18) while traveling for work, as well as in her personal life.

Proficiency:

Claire is a tech-savvy professional. Comfortable with web-based productivity tools, shared calendars, and collaborative platforms.

Attitudes and Behaviors:

Organized, detail-oriented, and efficiency-driven. Claire coordinates multiple trips throughout the year and values tools that simplify planning and keeping everyone informed. She dislikes excessive back-and-forth communication and prefers to send out important information digitally en masse to fellow team members.

Frustrations and Needs:

Coordinating schedules, communicating travel details, and keeping coworkers on the same page can be time-consuming and tiring. Claire needs a centralized platform where she can build a clear itinerary, and easily share updates with her team. She is frustrated by scattered information and excessive communication with her team (i.e. answering the same inquiries repeatedly).

Goals:

Claire wants to plan professional trips efficiently and wants to be able to share them easily so that every coworker has immediate access to the most up-to-date version of the itinerary.

User Story:

"As a corporate team planner and repeat user, I need to create and share travel itineraries in one place so that my coworkers can easily access trip details and stay informed about the schedule."

Use Case:

Claire is planning a company retreat to Mexico in December of 2026. Ten staff members, including herself, will be in attendance and she needs to produce an itinerary for 5 days of safe-for-work fun! Scenario:

Precondition:

Claire is currently on the home page of the travel itinerary website.

Postcondition:

Claire shares the final version of the itinerary with her team members.

Interaction Flow:

  • 1. Claire opens the travel itinerary website.
  • 2. The website displays its landing page with a “Log In” option in the upper navigation bar.
  • 3. Claire clicks “Log In” and enters her existing account credentials.
  • 4. The website authenticates Claire and displays her dashboard with her previous trips and saved itineraries.
  • 5. Claire clicks “Create New Trip.”
  • 6. The website displays options to create a trip from scratch or use an existing itinerary as a template.
  • 7. Claire selects “Use a Template.”
  • 8. The website displays her previously saved itineraries, including last year’s company retreat in Tulum, Mexico.
  • 9. Claire selects last year’s company retreat.
  • 10. The website displays a preview of the previous itinerary and an option to “Use This Template.”
  • 11. Claire clicks “Use This Template.”
  • 12. The website creates a copy of the previous itinerary (preserving the original) and opens an editable trip details form.
  • 13. Claire updates the trip name, the destination city in Mexico to Playa del Carmen, travel dates, and roster of attendees (to include new hires and remove employees who've left the company).
  • 14. The website saves the updated trip details and displays the itinerary editor with last year’s schedule.
  • 15. Claire reviews the existing schedule, removes outdated activities, modifies relevant events, and adds new activities for this year’s retreat.
  • 16. The website updates the itinerary and displays the revised schedule.
  • 17. Claire reviews the schedule and saves her changes.
  • 18. The website confirms that the itinerary has been saved and displays a preview of the completed trip.
  • 19. Claire clicks “Share” to distribute the itinerary to all her coworkers.
  • 20. The website displays sharing options, allowing Claire to enter email addresses or generate a shareable link and select access permissions.
  • Claire enters her coworkers’ email addresses, selects view-only access, and clicks “Send.”
  • The website sends the invitations and confirms that the itinerary has been shared successfully.
