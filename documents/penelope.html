Planner Penelope | Family Reunion Organizer

1. Persona

Name and Background

Name: Penelope Martinez
Age: 41
Occupation: Working Professional
Role: Family Reunion Organizer
Trip: Multigenerational Family Reunion
Group Size: 24 Family Members
Generations: Four

Penelope is an experienced traveler who enjoys planning meaningful experiences for her family. She has taken responsibility for organizing a reunion that brings together 24 relatives across four generations. She is comfortable researching destinations, comparing activities, coordinating reservations, and organizing schedules.

Although Penelope enjoys planning, she often becomes the primary source of information for everyone attending. Family members rely on her to answer questions about activity times, locations, costs, reservations, and changes to the schedule. She wants to spend less time managing logistics during the reunion and more time enjoying the experience with her family.

Family Composition

  • Penelope's Household (4): Penelope, 41; spouse, 43; children, 8 and 13.
  • Parents (2): Mother, 65; father, 68.
  • Grandparents (2): Grandmother, 79; grandfather, 82.
  • Sister's Household (5): Sister, 38; spouse, 40; children, 3, 6, and 10.
  • Brother's Household (4): Brother, 35; spouse, 34; children, 5 and 9.
  • Cousins' Household (7): Cousins, 28, 31, and 33; partners, 29 and 32; children, 7 and 12.

Family Dynamics

Penelope's family includes young children, teenagers, working adults, parents, and grandparents. Their interests, budgets, mobility needs, and energy levels vary. Some relatives prefer active outings, while others enjoy relaxed activities and opportunities to spend time together.

Penelope wants everyone to participate in important reunion events while offering optional activities that allow family members to choose experiences that fit their interests and needs. She also needs accurate attendance information, including children whose parents respond on their behalf.

2. Technology

Devices

  • Primary: Windows laptop using Google Chrome.
  • Secondary: iPhone using Safari while traveling.

Technology Proficiency

Penelope is comfortable using websites, email, online reservation systems, and digital calendars. She expects a straightforward interface that works well on both desktop and mobile devices.

Loves

  • Organizing memorable family experiences.
  • Having information available in one location.
  • Clear schedules and organized reservations.
  • Knowing who plans to attend each activity.
  • Giving family members access to current plans.

Hates

  • Answering the same logistical questions repeatedly.
  • Searching through emails for reservation details.
  • Tracking attendance through multiple group chats.
  • Communicating schedule changes individually.
  • Missing family moments because she is coordinating logistics.

Frustrations and Needs

Penelope's biggest frustration is becoming the central source of information for the reunion. Relatives repeatedly ask when dinner starts, where they are meeting, what activities are planned, and who is attending. Although she enjoys organizing the trip, answering these questions takes time away from participating in the experiences she planned.

Important information is scattered across reservation confirmations, emails, text messages, and personal notes. When a schedule changes, she needs a reliable way to update the itinerary and communicate the change to everyone attending.

Planning for four generations also requires attention to accessibility, age suitability, transportation, and cost. Penelope researches activities through external websites and manually adds her selections to the shared itinerary. She needs a simple way to distinguish important reunion events from optional activities without adding unnecessary planning tools.

Penelope needs one centralized platform to organize trip information, share activity details, collect RSVPs, and track attendance. Parents should be able to RSVP for their children without creating separate accounts. Most importantly, Penelope wants family members to find the information they need independently.

3. Attitudes and Behaviors

Penelope genuinely enjoys bringing her family together and believes shared experiences create lasting memories. As an experienced traveler, she begins planning early and prefers to establish the most important reunion events before researching additional activities. She is organized, detail-oriented, and comfortable making decisions on behalf of the group.

Because the reunion includes four generations, Penelope considers age, interests, accessibility, and budget when selecting activities. She researches options on external websites and looks for a combination of free, affordable, and premium experiences. She understands that not everyone will participate in every activity and wants family members to have flexibility without disrupting the overall plan.

Penelope enters selected activities into a shared itinerary, including dates, start and end times, locations, costs, reservation information, and important notes. She invites adult relatives by email and relies on their Going, Maybe, or Not Going responses to coordinate reservations. Parents manage RSVPs for their children, allowing Penelope to see an accurate attendance count for each activity.

During the reunion, Penelope uses her phone to check the schedule and update activities when plans change. She expects family members to access the current itinerary themselves and receive email notifications about changes. She enjoys being the person who brings everyone together, but she does not want that responsibility to prevent her from enjoying the reunion.

4. User Story

As a family reunion organizer, I want to create a shared itinerary so everyone can stay informed and coordinate their participation independently.

5. Use Case

Use Case Statement

As the organizer of a multigenerational family vacation, I need one place to organize and share our itinerary so everyone knows the plan without constantly asking me for updates.

Scenario

Penelope is organizing the Martinez Family Reunion for 24 family members across four generations. She needs to create a trip, organize important reunion events, and add optional activities suitable for different ages, interests, abilities, and budgets.

She researches attractions on external websites, compares options, and manually enters her selected activities into the trip planner. Each activity includes a date, required start and end times, location, estimated cost, notes, and an RSVP requirement. She also stores reservation information when it becomes available.

Penelope invites adult relatives through email. Each adult accepts the invitation and accesses the shared trip. Parents can add their children as household members and submit Going, Maybe, or Not Going responses on their behalf. Children do not need separate accounts.

As responses arrive, Penelope reviews attendance totals directly within the trip itinerary. She uses these totals to coordinate reservations. If an activity changes, she updates the event and the website sends email notifications to the adult travelers while displaying the latest information in the itinerary.

Preconditions

  • Penelope has access to the trip-planning website.
  • She has a valid email address and can create an account.
  • She knows the reunion destination, dates, and general group composition.
  • She has the email addresses of the adults she plans to invite.

Postconditions

  • The Martinez Family Reunion is saved and accessible through My Trips.
  • Core events and optional activities appear in chronological order.
  • Adult relatives can access the itinerary after accepting their invitations.
  • Parents can add children to their household and manage their RSVPs.
  • Activities collect Going, Maybe, or Not Going responses when RSVP is enabled.
  • Penelope can review individual attendance totals, including children, within the trip itinerary.
  • Updated activity details are reflected in the shared itinerary and communicated to adult travelers through email.

6. Detailed User Interaction Flow

The following interaction flow describes how Penelope creates and manages her family reunion and how invited adult relatives participate. The flow uses the approved eight-page website structure. Sign Up and Log In are separate authentication screens and do not count toward the eight-page limit.

Account Creation

Step 1: Create an Account

Penelope: Opens the website and selects Sign Up.

Website: Displays the registration form with the following fields:

  • First Name
  • Last Name
  • Email Address
  • Password
  • Confirm Password
  • Sign Up button

Penelope: Enters her personal information and submits the form.

Website: Validates the required fields, email format, and password confirmation. Invalid information produces a field-specific error message. Valid information creates her account.

Step 2: Open My Trips

Penelope: Completes registration.

Website: Opens My Trips, displaying trips she organizes and trips she attends. Because she has no trips yet, the list is empty. A Create New Trip button is available.

Create the Family Reunion

Step 3: Start a New Trip

Penelope: Selects Create New Trip.

Website: Opens the Create Trip form with:

  • Trip Name
  • Trip Type
  • Destination
  • Start Date
  • End Date
  • Trip Description
  • Save Trip button
  • Cancel button

Step 4: Enter Reunion Details

Penelope: Enters Martinez Family Reunion as the trip name, selects Family Reunion as the trip type, enters the destination, chooses the arrival and departure dates, and adds a description of the multigenerational gathering.

She selects Save Trip.

Website: Checks the required fields and verifies that the end date is not earlier than the start date. If information is invalid, the form displays an error and allows correction. If valid, the trip is saved and Penelope receives the Organizer role.

Step 5: Review Trip Itinerary

Penelope: Opens her newly created reunion.

Website: Displays the trip itinerary with the reunion name, destination, dates, description, and an empty activity schedule.

As Organizer, Penelope can select Add Activity, Edit Trip, or Travelers & Invites. The itinerary also contains an organizer-only attendance summary.

Organize Core Reunion Events

Step 6: Open Add Activity

Penelope: Selects Add Activity to schedule the family welcome dinner.

Website: Opens the Add Activity form with:

  • Activity Name
  • Activity Type: Core or Optional
  • Activity Date
  • Start Time
  • End Time
  • Location Name
  • Location Address
  • Description
  • Estimated Cost
  • Cost Notes
  • Event Notes
  • Reservation Confirmation
  • Reservation Link
  • RSVP Required: Yes or No
  • Save Activity button
  • Cancel button

All activities are visible to accepted trip travelers. There is no subgroup visibility feature.

Step 7: Add Welcome Dinner

Penelope: Enters Family Welcome Dinner, selects Core Event, and chooses Friday evening for the date.

She enters the required start and end times, restaurant name, address, description, estimated cost, and relevant notes. She includes reservation information if available and enables RSVPs.

She selects Save Activity.

Website: Validates the required information, confirms that the date falls within the trip, and checks that the end time is later than the start time.

If validation fails, the form displays an error. If valid, the activity is saved and displayed on the trip itinerary.

Step 8: Add Main Family Gathering

Penelope: Selects Add Activity again. She enters Main Family Gathering, chooses Core Event, and enters the date, required start and end times, location, address, description, and estimated cost.

She includes notes about accessible parking, food, outdoor seating, and what relatives should bring. She enables RSVPs and saves.

Website: Validates the information, saves the event, and adds it to the itinerary in date order.

Step 9: Add Family Photograph

Penelope: Opens Add Activity and enters Family Photograph as a Core Event.

She selects the date, required start and end times, meeting location, address, and any applicable cost.

In Event Notes, she includes arrival instructions, meeting location details, and suggested clothing. She enables RSVPs and saves the activity.

Website: Validates and saves the photograph event, then returns her to the updated itinerary.

Research and Add Optional Activities

Step 10: Research Activities Externally

Penelope: Visits external travel and attraction websites to compare activities appropriate for children, teenagers, adults, and grandparents.

She considers admission costs, accessibility, travel distance, duration, and group discounts.

Website: Does not provide activity discovery, search filters, recommendations, or booking tools. Penelope manually enters her selections through Add Activity.

Step 11: Add an Optional Activity

Penelope: Returns to the reunion itinerary and selects Add Activity.

Website: Opens the Add Activity form.

Penelope: Enters the selected attraction, chooses Optional Activity, and completes the date, required start and end times, location, address, description, estimated cost, and notes.

She includes age suitability, accessibility details, child pricing, group discounts, and reservation information where applicable. She enables RSVPs and selects Save Activity.

Website: Validates and saves the activity, then displays it in chronological order on the shared itinerary.

Step 12: Complete Activity Schedule

Penelope: Repeats the process for additional optional activities, including free, affordable, and premium experiences suitable for different family members.

Website: Displays all Core Events and Optional Activities together in chronological order. Penelope can open and edit individual activities.

Invite Adult Family Members

Step 13: Open Travelers & Invites

Penelope: Selects Travelers & Invites from the reunion itinerary.

Website: Displays the traveler roster with accepted and pending invitation statuses and an Invite Traveler button.

Step 14: Send Email Invitations

Penelope: Selects Invite Traveler.

Website: Opens the invitation form with:

  • Adult Traveler Email Address
  • Add Another Email button
  • Invitation Message
  • Send Invitations button
  • Cancel button

Penelope: Enters the adult relatives' email addresses and adds a message asking parents to register their children as household members.

She selects Send Invitations.

Website: Validates the email addresses, sends invitations containing an acceptance link, and marks the invitations as Pending.

Step 15: Accept Trip Invitation

Family Member: An invited adult opens the invitation link from their email.

Website: Opens Accept Invitation, identifies the Martinez Family Reunion, and prompts the recipient to Sign Up or Log In.

Family Member: Creates an account if necessary, or logs in with an existing account, then accepts the invitation.

Website: Associates the adult with the trip, assigns the Traveler role, changes the invitation status to Accepted, and provides access through My Trips.

Manage Children Within Households

Step 16: Add Household Members

Family Member: A parent opens Travelers & Invites and selects Add Household Member.

Website: Displays a household section with the following fields:

  • Child's Name
  • Child's Age: Optional
  • Add Another Child button
  • Save Household button
  • Cancel button

Family Member: Enters each child's name and optional age, then selects Save Household.

Website: Saves the children as travelers associated with the parent's account. Children do not receive email invitations, login credentials, or separate accounts.

Step 17: Review Traveler Roster

Penelope: Opens Travelers & Invites to review family participation.

Website: Displays adult travelers, their invitation statuses, and children associated with each household.

The roster counts adults and children as individual travelers without requiring an account for every child.

Collect Household RSVPs

Step 18: Open Activity Details

Family Member: An adult opens Martinez Family Reunion from My Trips and selects an activity from the trip itinerary.

Website: Opens Activity Details & RSVP and displays:

  • Activity Name
  • Activity Date
  • Start and End Times
  • Location and Address
  • Description
  • Estimated Cost
  • Cost Notes
  • Event Notes
  • Reservation Information
  • Host Contact
  • RSVP Options, if required

Step 19: Select Household RSVPs

Website: Checks whether RSVP Required is enabled for the activity. If not, the traveler can review the event information without submitting an RSVP.

If RSVPs are required, the website displays the adult and their registered children. Each person has the following response options:

  • Going
  • Maybe
  • Not Going

Family Member: Selects an individual response for themselves and each child attending the reunion.

For example, a parent may select Going for themselves and two children and Not Going for another child.

Any traveler without a saved response remains Not Responded.

Step 20: Submit and Confirm RSVPs

Family Member: Reviews the household responses and selects Submit RSVPs.

Website: Saves a separate RSVP record for each person, updates attendance totals, and displays a confirmation message.

The confirmation includes an Add to Calendar option and host contact information. The parent can return later to update their household's responses.

Review Attendance and Confirm Reservations

Step 21: Review Attendance Summary

Penelope: Opens the trip itinerary to review attendance.

Website: Displays an organizer-only attendance summary within the trip itinerary.

Each activity shows the number of individual travelers marked Going, Maybe, Not Going, or Not Responded. Children are included in these totals.

Penelope can review the responses to determine the expected headcount for reservations.

Step 22: Finalize Reservations

Penelope: Uses the attendance totals to contact a restaurant or activity provider and confirm the reservation.

She selects Edit Activity to add or update the reservation confirmation, booking link, and arrival instructions.

Website: Opens the existing activity information for editing. Once Penelope saves, the updated details appear in the itinerary.

Manage Changes During the Reunion

Step 23: Access the Mobile Itinerary

Penelope: Opens the website on her iPhone and selects Martinez Family Reunion from My Trips.

Website: Displays a mobile-friendly itinerary with activities in chronological order.

Step 24: Edit an Activity

Penelope: Learns that the main family gathering must begin 30 minutes later.

She opens the activity, selects Edit Activity, changes the Start Time, adjusts End Time if needed, and selects Save Activity.

Website: Validates the updated times and saves the changes. The current schedule immediately appears in the shared itinerary.

Step 25: Notify Family Members

Website: Sends an email notification to accepted adult travelers informing them that the activity details have changed.

The email identifies the updated event and directs travelers to the current itinerary for details.

Family Member: Opens the email or signs in to the website and reviews the updated event.

Website: Displays the revised time, location, and event notes. Parents can share this information with their children without requiring children to use the website.

Final Outcome

Step 26: Enjoy the Reunion

Penelope: Participates in the reunion knowing that adult relatives can independently review activity details, RSVP for their households, and access current schedules.

Website: Maintains one shared itinerary, tracks individual attendance, provides reservation details, and communicates activity changes through email.

Penelope successfully organizes the Martinez Family Reunion using one centralized website. Adult family members can access the schedule and RSVP for themselves and their children. Accurate attendance totals help Penelope coordinate reservations, while shared information and email updates reduce repetitive logistical questions. Penelope can focus on spending time with her family instead of managing every detail.
