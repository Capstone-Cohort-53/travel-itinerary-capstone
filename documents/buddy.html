Birthday Party Persona

Capstone Project Documentation

Purpose

The purpose of this site is to give party guests one simple, mobile-friendly place to find everything they need for the birthday party, including the date, time, location, theme, and RSVP, so the host does not have to answer the same questions over and over.

Audience

This site is designed for the friends, family members, and coworkers invited to the birthday party. Most guests will open the site on a smartphone from a text message or social media link. They have a wide range of ages and technical comfort levels, so the site must be easy to read and quick to use.

Goal

The goal is for at least 90% of invited guests to RSVP through the site within one week of receiving the link, and for the host to receive fewer than five follow-up questions about basic party details.

Persona

Name

Buddy Delgado

User Story

Buddy is a 34-year-old elementary school teacher and a close friend of the birthday guest. He uses his iPhone for nearly everything and checks the site in Safari, usually between classes or while commuting. He is friendly and organized, replies to invites quickly, and likes knowing the plan ahead of time. He will open the site from a text message link and expects to find the details in under a minute.

Technology

  • iPhone with the Safari browser
  • Text messaging and email for invites and reminders
  • Social media apps, mostly to see friends' posts and events
  • Calendar app on his phone to keep track of plans

Devices

  • Primary: iPhone, used for nearly everything
  • Secondary: school laptop, used only occasionally for personal tasks

Loves

  • Seeing the date, time, and address right away
  • RSVPing in a few taps
  • Adding the event to his calendar with one button
  • Knowing ahead of time what to bring and what to wear

Hates

  • Long forms with questions that are not needed
  • Pages that load slowly or have tiny text
  • Having to text the host to get basic details
  • Creating an account that takes more than a minute

Proficiency

  • Smartphone use: High
  • Web browsing and links: Medium to high
  • Online forms and accounts: Medium
  • Troubleshooting technical problems: Low to medium

Attitudes and Behaviors

  • Friendly and organized, and likes knowing the plan ahead of time
  • Replies to invites quickly and expects the same ease from the site
  • Checks his phone between classes and while commuting, so he skims instead of reading every word
  • Expects to find what he needs in under a minute, or he will text the host instead
  • Plans around the event, such as arranging a sitter, so he needs start and end times

Frustrations

  • Invites that bury the date, time, or address
  • Pages that load slowly or are hard to read on a phone
  • Not knowing whether to bring a gift, food, or a plus-one
  • Having to text the host to ask basic questions

Goals

  • Quickly see when and where the party is
  • RSVP easily
  • Find out the theme, dress code, and gift or registry information

Additional Information Needed

  • Parking or transit directions
  • Food and dietary information, including vegetarian and allergy-friendly options
  • Host contact information for last-minute questions
  • Start and end times so he can arrange a sitter

Interaction Flow

  • User: Open Link from text message
  • Website: Checks whether the user is already logged in
  • Website: If not logged in, shows the log in or sign up page
  • User: Buddy doesn't have account, chooses to sign up and clicks sign up
  • Website: Shows him the sign-up page
  • User: Inputs all his information and clicks create account
  • Website: Makes him an account with the information he supplied and shows him list of events
  • User: Click on the event he was invited to
  • Website: Opens the event and shares the details: theme, food, parking, gifts, date, time, place
  • User: Buddy scrolls and reads, decides he wants to go
  • User: Clicks on the RSVP
  • Website: Shows the RSVP form with name, guests, dietary needs
  • User: Fills out the form and clicks Submit
  • Website: Shows a confirmation summary, an add-to-calendar option, and the host's contact information
  • User: buddy sees it and finished up

Use-Case Scenario

Buddy is between classes when he gets a text message from the host with a link to the birthday party site. He taps the link, and the site opens in Safari on his iPhone. He does not have an account yet, so he taps Sign up, enters his name, email, and a password, and lands on his list of events. He opens the birthday party and sees the date, time, place, theme, food, parking, and gift information. He decides he wants to go, taps RSVP, and fills out his name, number of guests, and dietary needs. The site shows a confirmation with a summary, an add-to-calendar button, and the host's contact information. He adds the party to his calendar and puts his phone away, all in under a minute.

Precondition

  • Buddy has been invited and has received the party link in a text message
  • Buddy has an iPhone with Safari and an internet connection
  • The party event and its details have been created by the host
  • Buddy has not yet responded to the invitation

Postcondition

  • Buddy has an account on the site
  • Buddy's RSVP, including guests and dietary needs, is saved
  • The host can see Buddy's response without sending a text
  • Buddy has a confirmation with the party details and the host's contact information
  • The party is on Buddy's calendar if he chose to add it
